# Locations

This file is the index of canonical locations.

For major locations, create a dedicated file later if necessary.

Locations should behave like persistent places:

- businesses change ownership;
- streets acquire reputations;
- buildings can be damaged;
- seasons affect travel;
- residents move;
- laws change;
- characters remember previous events.

Do not reset a location merely because a new POV character enters it.

## Example only — location

> **NON-CANONICAL EXAMPLE.**

## South Gate Market

### Metadata

- Location ID: LOC-SGM-001
- Type: public market district
- Region: southern edge of the capital
- First appearance: Story 1
- Current status: active

### Physical description

A dense commercial district built around the old southern gate.

The original defensive wall still divides several streets, forcing foot traffic through narrow archways.

Shops have been built directly against older stonework.

### Sensory identity

- Bakers start before dawn.
- Delivery carts make the gate unbearably noisy at first light.
- Rainwater carries flour, ash and horse manure into the same drains.
- Legal ward-lines glow faintly blue under certain paving stones after sunset.

### Social identity

Used by:

- tradespeople;
- municipal workers;
- travellers;
- lower and middle-income residents;
- wholesale buyers.

Wealthy residents may visit specialist shops but generally do not linger.

### Important sub-locations

#### Corra's Bakery

- narrow corner shop;
- owner knows many municipal workers;
- useful background continuity location.

#### South Gate Registry Office

- handles travel documents and commercial permits;
- queue forms outdoors;
- excellent setting for minor bureaucratic crossover between otherwise unrelated characters.

### Story events

#### Story 1

- POV character buys breakfast here.
- A registry closure is mentioned.

#### Potential later use

- another POV experiences the same closure as a serious personal problem rather than background inconvenience.

### Canonical details that must not drift

- The old gate remains standing.
- The Registry Office is on the east side of the gate.
- Corra's Bakery is opposite the registry lane, not adjacent to it.
