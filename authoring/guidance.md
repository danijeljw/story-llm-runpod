# Authoring conventions

Keep fictional facts within their owning series. Distinguish objective canon from character belief, rumours, plans and worked examples. Approved prose and deliberate edits are durable sources; raw generation requires review before promotion.

Use one POV per scene unless the story deliberately calls for another form. Record consequences in the series bible when they become approved canon. A new series may define its own voice, genre and narrative form; the working world's fantasy guide is specific to that continuity.

Use the reusable [character guidance](characters.md) and [templates](templates/README.md). Keep story-local characters in that story's `characters/<slug>/` directory; promote a recurring character to the series bible without retaining competing canonical copies.
